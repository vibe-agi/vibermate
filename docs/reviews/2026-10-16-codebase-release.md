The retained-authority boundary remains precise: routing stays frozen in `captureassignment`; repositories decide current permission. Withdrawal cancels and joins owned work, but cannot retract bytes already admitted, buffered or sent. A blind tunnel is one admitted operation. A terminally withdrawn receipt cannot be revived by later renewal; a fresh authenticated connection can obtain fresh authority. Cleanup that has not returned remains owned even when a public shutdown deadline expires.
