# Release ViberMate under AGPLv3 with a separate commercial license

Status: proposed pending copyright-holder confirmation and legal review.

ViberMate's project-owned code is released under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0. Organizations may use the open-source version commercially when
they follow the AGPLv3. A separate Commercial License is offered only for
customers that need rights outside the AGPLv3, such as closed-source
integration or additional contractual permissions. Professional services are a
separate offering and do not restrict use of the AGPLv3 version.

Contributions are accepted under `CLA.md` so contributors retain copyright
while the maintainer receives the permissions needed to distribute the same
project under AGPLv3 and separate commercial terms. Third-party components
remain under their own licenses; a commercial distribution must preserve those
notices and cannot grant rights the maintainer does not hold.

This is a licensing policy decision, not legal advice. The copyright holder,
contribution process, commercial agreement, and release SBOM require review by
qualified counsel before commercial delivery.
